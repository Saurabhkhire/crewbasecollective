Step by step

How to submit

📦 What to include tick them off as you go (saved in this browser)

    🎬 Demo: video, live, or both

    You need at least one way for judges to see your project working. Both is best.

    🎥 Demo video1–3 minutes. Upload to YouTube, Loom or Vimeo and paste the link. Make sure it's public or unlisted.
    🌐 Live demoA URL judges can open, or show it running at your table during demos. Keep it running until judging ends.
    ⭐ BothA live link plus a short video as backup, in case Wi-Fi or an API has a bad moment.

    💡 Make the demo land

    • Open with the agents doing something on screen: moving, talking, deciding.
    • Show the decision log: what each agent decided and why.
    • Run it twice if the outcome changes: that proves the agents decide.
    • Say which track you picked and which sponsor tools you used.

    FAQ

    Quick answers

    Where do we submit?

    Only on hackathons.crewbasecollective.com. One submission per team.

    When is the deadline?

    It's announced at the event and shown on the submission site. Demos happen before the event wraps at 8:30 PM.

    Do we need a video if we demo live?

    No. You need a demo video, a live demo link, or both. A short video is a good backup.

    Do we have to use the sponsor tools?

    No, they're optional. If you use them, say which ones and how in your submission. Try them first in the Sponsor Labs.

    How big can a team be?

    Solo or up to 4 people. Pick one track per project.

    Something else?

    Ask the organisers at the event.

    Ready to ship?

    Open the submission site, fill in your project, add your demo video or live link, and you're done.
