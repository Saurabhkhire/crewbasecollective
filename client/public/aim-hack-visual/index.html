    The challenge

    From one-to-one, to many-to-many.

    Most AI today is one human talking to one assistant. The next step is multiplayer: many agents interacting with each other and with humans, where the outcome isn't scripted. It emerges from what the agents decide.

    Today: 1 human ↔ 1 assistant

    You ask, it answers. A text box, one voice, and a script that's predictable by design.

    Here: many agents ↔ each other ↔ humans

    Visible characters with their own goals, personalities and minds that think, act, move and decide. They shape a film, a game, or a real person's growth.
